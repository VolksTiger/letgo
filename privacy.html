Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 25, 2026

At LetGo, we believe your emotional wellness journey should be completely private. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information:

  • No Account Required: You don't need to create an account to use LetGo. We don't collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers.
  • Local Storage Only: All your journal entries, reflections, and emotional data are stored locally on your device. We never upload this data to our servers.
  • Anonymous Analytics: We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (like app opens, feature usage) to improve the app. This data cannot be linked to you personally.

2. How We Use Your Information

The anonymous analytics we collect are used solely to:

  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Understand which features are most helpful
  • Guide future development decisions

3. Data Storage and Security

  • On-Device Storage: Your personal content (journal entries, reflections, breathing exercises) is stored exclusively on your device using iOS's secure storage mechanisms.
  • No Cloud Sync: We do not sync your data to any cloud services.
  • Encryption: Data stored on your device is protected by iOS's built-in encryption when your device is locked.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties.

Since we don't collect personal data, there's nothing to share. The anonymous analytics we collect may be processed by third-party analytics providers (such as Apple's App Analytics), but this data is anonymized and aggregated.

5. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA, and Other Regulations)

We are committed to complying with global privacy regulations including:

  • GDPR (EU): You have the right to access, rectify, and delete your data. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control.
  • CCPA (California): We do not sell personal information. You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app.
  • Other Jurisdictions: We respect privacy rights under all applicable laws.

6. Children's Privacy

LetGo is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
